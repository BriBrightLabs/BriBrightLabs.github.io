Privacy Policy

Marble Workshop, by BriBright Labs · Last updated 13 October 2026

Marble Workshop is a marble-run building game. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect. It is written to be read by a parent, not a lawyer.

The short version

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you or your child. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers that receive your data. The only outside service the app contacts is Google AdMob, which displays the banner advertisement.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following on the device itself, where only the app can read it:

  • Boards you build and save
  • Your score
  • Your sound and colour settings

This information never leaves the device. It is not backed up to us, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently.

Advertising

Marble Workshop displays a banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To serve that advertisement, AdMob may receive your device's advertising identifier along with general technical information such as device type, operating system version, and approximate region derived from your network address.

Because this app is directed to children, we have configured AdMob to treat every request as child-directed. In practice this means:

  • Advertisements are non-personalised. They are not selected based on your child's interests or browsing history.
  • Advertising content is limited to a General maturity rating.
  • We have enabled the under-age-of-consent flag, which restricts how Google may process the request.

We do not receive any individual information from AdMob. We can see only aggregate counts of how many advertisements were shown.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy & Terms and in the AdMob policy documentation.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for children and is operated in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is no chat, no messaging, no user-generated content shared between players, no social media integration, and no way for a child to contact anyone through the app.

Permissions

The app requests internet access, which is used solely to load the banner advertisement. It does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, precise location, or storage outside its own sandbox.

Purchases

The app currently contains no in-app purchases. If an optional ad-removal purchase is added in a future version, it will be processed entirely by Google Play. We would never receive or store payment details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
